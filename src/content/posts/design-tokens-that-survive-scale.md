## Why semantic roles matter

Primitive colors drift quickly across products. Semantic tokens keep intent stable and preserve intent when themes evolve.

## Migration strategy

1. Keep old token aliases during transition.
2. Move components to semantic roles.
3. Remove legacy aliases after adoption.

## Practical advice

Start with typography and surface tokens first, then migrate component-specific states.
